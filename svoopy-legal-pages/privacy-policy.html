This Privacy Policy applies to the Svoopy app and related services. Our contact details are listed at the end of this policy.

2. Information we process

Account and profile information

We process information you provide when creating or updating an account, such as your name, email address, age, and profile image. You can sign in with email and password or Google Sign-In. Firebase Authentication supports account authentication; we do not receive your Google password through Google Sign-In.

Profile location

If you choose to add or update your profile location using the map, the app may use your device’s location in the foreground to help you select a location. The address-level location saved to your profile is visible to all Svoopy users. This feature is not intended to track your location continuously in the background.

Location visibility: Only save a profile location you are comfortable showing to every Svoopy user. Do not use it for sensitive information you do not want to share.

Chats and attachments

Chat messages are stored using Firebase Cloud Firestore so conversations can be delivered and displayed to their participants. Photos, files, or other attachments you send may be stored using Firebase Storage.

Messages are intended to be visible to participants in the relevant conversation within Svoopy. The admin panel does not provide routine browsing of private chat content. Svoopy does not claim that chats are end-to-end encrypted, and we cannot promise that authorized technical access is impossible.

Calls, shows, and live streams

  • Calls: Audio and video calls are provided through ZEGOCLOUD. Svoopy does not record calls.
  • Scheduled shows: Scheduled shows are recorded for replay. If you appear or speak in a recorded show, your image, voice, or other contribution may be included.
  • Spontaneous live streams: A spontaneous live stream is recorded only when its creator chooses to record it. If recording is enabled, your participation may be included.

A show replay may be offered for purchase while it remains active. The administrator sets the replay-access period. Once a purchaser’s access period expires, a new purchase may be required to watch again. A creator can contact the administrator to request removal of a show or replay.

Wallet, payments, and withdrawals

Wallet transactions are denominated in euros. Stripe processes wallet top-ups. We may process transaction details needed to update your wallet, such as the amount, currency, payment status, and transaction reference.

If you request a withdrawal, we process the bank-account details you provide so an approved transfer can be made. Your bank details are visible to you and authorized Svoopy administrators. The minimum withdrawal request is €100. An administrator reviews withdrawal requests; an approved transfer is normally processed within 7–10 working days.

Notifications and technical information

Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device notification token and related delivery information. Firebase and other providers may process technical, diagnostic, and service-operation information needed to provide and secure the app.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage accounts and profiles;
  • provide sign-in, chat, calls, live streams, scheduled shows, replays, and push notifications;
  • display the profile details and location that users choose to share;
  • process wallet top-ups, transaction records, and eligible withdrawal requests;
  • respond to support, safety, content-removal, and account-deletion requests;
  • maintain service security, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Visibility and sharing

  • Profile location: The address-level location saved to your profile is visible to all Svoopy users.
  • Other profile details: Your name and profile image may be shown to users who can view your profile in the app.
  • Chats: Messages are intended for participants in the relevant conversation. They are stored with Firebase and are not represented as end-to-end encrypted.
  • Shows and live streams: Recorded content may be made available to eligible users for replay under the purchase terms and access period shown in the app.
  • Administrators: Authorized administrators may process account, wallet, bank, and support information to operate Svoopy, review withdrawals, moderate accounts, or handle requests.

We may also disclose information to service providers described below, when required by law, to protect users or Svoopy, or with your direction.
